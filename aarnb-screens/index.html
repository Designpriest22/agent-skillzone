Text From Your Ex

The screen turns into a giant phone. A chat thread types itself out in real time, and every reply is an R&B lyric the crowd sings along to. It ends on a final bubble: “u coming to All About R&B tonight?” with the heart as the read receipt.

Crowd
Lyric replies are a built-in sing-along. A QR code lets the crowd send in their own “texts” to run as shout-outs between sets.
Why
R&B lives on late-night texts. The joke lands instantly and can be swapped for every artist's catalogue.

The Dedication Mixtape

A cassette spins in close-up and the J-card is handwritten. As the tape spools, crowd dedications scroll on the label: “For Tolu, from Ada”, “To my 2009 self”. Then the tape flips to Side B and reveals the next artist.

Crowd
Dedications are collected on the ticketing page or at a booth in the venue, then batched into the loop before doors open.
Why
Nostalgia sells throwback R&B nights. The Side B flip also works as a set-change interstitial.

Every loop is drawn in code from the supplied logo files and colours (red, cream, orchid, ink, white), so text, dates and artist names can be changed and the loops re-rendered at any size. Motion is timed to 96 BPM, a mid-tempo R&B pocket, so cuts land on the beat if the screens are synced to the DJ.
