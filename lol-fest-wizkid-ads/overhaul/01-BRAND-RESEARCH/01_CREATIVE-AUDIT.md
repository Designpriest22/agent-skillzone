# Creative audit of the previous sponsor assets (v1)

I reviewed every v1 sponsor asset frame by frame: 39 videos of 15 s each and 39 static flyers. The verdict is that the work is technically clean, with correct copy, logos and QC, but creatively weak. The brief calls for a rebuild, not a revision.

| # | Problem in v1 | Evidence | v2 decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One template with 13 skins.** Every spot ran intro → footage with a corner bug → the same stacked lockup. Every flyer used the same WIZKID-over-LOL-over-date stack. Only the background changed. | The storyboards line up beat for beat across sponsors. The flyer contact sheet shows 13 near-identical columns. | Each sponsor gets its own **signature device** that drives the opening, the transitions, the footage frame, the CTA shape and the closing card. Flyer layouts differ by brand, and each sponsor gets 2 distinct concepts. |
| 2 | **Openings were logos appearing on flat colour.** That is the textbook "logo on black" opener. | Most 0–1 s frames showed an empty field with a logo fading in. | Motion starts on frame 1, and the brand device fills the frame. The logo is earned by the animation, not faded in. |
| 3 | **The footage was underused.** Shots were short, dark and cut on an invented 15 s grid with no relation to the music. Event text sat on top of the best moments. | The arrival and shadow shots read as black. The pyro moment appeared for under 2 s in most spots. | The edit is **cut to the supplied track** ("Final (Baba Nla)", 107.5 BPM): cuts on downbeats, a speed ramp into each hit, a punch zoom on the arms-out pyro moment, and a freeze frame carrying type. Dark shots get an exposure lift or are dropped, and the shadow shot is dropped entirely. |
| 4 | **Weak typography and hierarchy.** The venue and ticket lines ran 14–24 px on 1080 frames, with the same Anton/Oswald stack and centre alignment everywhere. | At phone size (360 px wide) the venue and ticket lines are unreadable. | A type scale with a clear hero: WIZKID 25–30 % of frame width minimum, the date ≥ 64 px, the venue ≥ 34 px and the CTA URL ≥ 44 px. Each brand gets a pairing (for example serif italic for premium spirits and hotels, heavy grotesk for party and youth brands) while the event information keeps the flyer's typography. |
| 5 | **Repetitive transitions.** Every spot used the same shape wipe at the same 0.4 s pace, with no motion blur. | | Transitions come from each brand's DNA (star iris, ribbon wave, poster slash, crest ring, X-bars, label window, hotel doors, woven strips, wing sweep, plus window, square tiles, starburst, amber pour). Fast moves get **real sub-frame motion blur** and every hit gets a camera shake. |
| 6 | **The sponsor was not integrated.** The brand appeared as a logo plus a colour, and the middle of each spot was "generic festival with a bug". | | The brand device stays on screen through the festival section. Footage plays inside the brand's shape, and the grade is built from the brand palette. The CTA button takes the brand's form (a boarding pass for the airline, a label plaque for the gin, a crest seal for BEST). |
| 7 | **The ticket CTA was small and late.** It was a footnote under the date. | | There is a mandatory **CTA button** with WIZKID.TIX.AFRICA, held ≥ 3 s inside 00:10–00:15, sized for mobile. It never appears in the final logo-only 5 s. |
| 8 | **The colours were inconsistent.** Several palettes were guessed. | | Palettes are sampled from the supplied logos plus published brand references (Coca-Cola #F40009, Heineken #008200 / #205527). Where a brand cannot be verified, the palette comes only from its own logo file, and that is flagged. |
| 9 | **Social compositions were cramped.** The square lockups crushed the logos, and the stories left the hero small. | | Each format is laid out from scratch with measured layout, so nothing overlaps. Interface safe zones are respected (9:16: top 210 px, bottom 470 px, right 160 px). |
| 10 | **The closing frame was mixed.** v1 put the festival, artist and date together with the sponsor. | | The v2 brief makes **00:15–00:20 the sponsor logo only**, on a crafted brand background with subtle motion. It contains no text, no LOL logo and no event information, and this is audit-enforced. |

## Process upgrades
- I evaluated the `mort1d/motion-graphics` skill (MIT, no packages, Node/ffmpeg/Chrome) and adopted its production rules: beat grid, three concepts with one chosen, motion from frame one, real motion blur, cut on motion, a 26 px minimum text size, critique rounds on stills, and an anti-generic list. Its automated installer was blocked by the session's safety policy, so its guides were applied by hand and no third-party code was executed.
- I applied the `graphic-designer` skill's rules to the flyers: three-level hierarchy, 60/30/10 colour, at most 2 families and 3 weights each, deliberate whitespace, and one alignment per composition.
- An automated content audit runs on every asset: approved copy only, only the sponsor's own logo, CTA present in 0–15 s, a logo-only closing, no footage in flyers, and the supplied photograph present in flyers.
