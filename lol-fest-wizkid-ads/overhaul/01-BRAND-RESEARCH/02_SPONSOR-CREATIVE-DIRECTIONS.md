## Flyer direction: revised after client review (supersedes the Flyer V1 / V2 columns above)

The client rejected portraits clipped into shapes (stars, rings, arches, windows and so on) and supplied a colour cut-out of Wizkid plus the completed LOL FEST flyer as the reference. Every v2 flyer now follows that flyer's own grammar:

- **One full, unclipped portrait:** the supplied colour cut-out (red corduroy jacket), never masked into a shape. Only the edge of the canvas may crop it.
- **Headline:** a thin condensed WIZKID headline sits behind the head. It is tracked out to width and never stretched.
- **Date and city:** 13 / DEC is set on the left and LIVE / ABUJA (ABUJA in a bold accent) on the right, both in front of the portrait and clear of the face.
- **Festival logo:** the LIVE OUT LOUD MUSIC FESTIVAL logo sits on the chest.
- **Bottom block:** EAGLE SQUARE, ABUJA, then the ticket line GET TICKETS AT WIZKID.TIX.AFRICA, then the **single sponsor logo** where the reference carries its partner row.
- **Brand devices:** these sit only behind the portrait, in the background.

**Legibility floors (audit-enforced)**
- URL ≥ 40 px (≥ 36 px on the 1:1 Brand Edition).
- Venue ≥ 28 px.
- Date numerals cap height ≥ 80 px.
- Sponsor logo ≥ 40 px tall. Logos are sized by equal optical area, so wide wordmarks and compact crests read at the same weight.
