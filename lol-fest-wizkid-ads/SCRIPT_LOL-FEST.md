# LOL FEST × WIZKID — direction & script
## "The poster, in motion."

The real LOL FEST flyer identity — **oxblood ground, the gold festival mark, the elegant tall display** — brought to life as **motion graphics**. Type reveals and builds on the beat; the WIZKID wordmark is lit by a travelling light‑sweep; the gold LIVE OUT LOUD logo inflates; the orange zigzag draws across. Real Wizkid footage is cut in for energy and grounded into the oxblood grade. Loud, gold, world‑class — and clean (no clutter, no AI, sponsors untouched).

*Why this and not the first pass:* the first direction imposed a generic black "brutalist" look with heavy fonts and fake editorial labels (board numbers, FX tags, 01/02/03). That's templated AI slop and it fought the brand. This direction is derived from the event's **own** artwork.

## System (all 4 ads)

**Colour** — Oxblood `#2B070E` · Maroon `#5A1420` · Gold `#F2C78C` · Cream `#F4EEE9` · Orange `#E6531A`.

**Type — one elegant voice, used big**
- **WIZKID** = the official gold wordmark (image), the headline.
- **Oswald Extra‑Light (200/300)** = the tall, thin, elegant display — `DECEMBER 13, 2026`, `13 DEC`, `LIVE`, `EAGLE SQUARE, ABUJA`.
- **LIVE OUT LOUD** = the gold festival mark, shown **once**, early.
- No heavy grotesques, no condensed-brutalist slabs, no metadata chrome.

**Motion vocabulary** (on the beat): mask‑reveal wipe · kinetic type rise · logo inflate + gold light‑sweep · zigzag draw‑on · speed ramp / freeze on hits · footage push‑ins · grain. Rhythmic, not constant.

**Rules:** approved copy only · LOL logo once · tickets `WIZKID.TIX.AFRICA` held ≥3 s · final 4–5 s all 16 sponsors (Coca‑Cola first, equal optical area, **no effects on logos**) · real footage only, no AI Wizkid · song rights cleared before paid use.

**Four ads = one living identity** (same oxblood‑gold system; the clip, song section and motion emphasis change):
1. **Money & Love** — title-led, elegant kinetic build (this one).
2. — second clip, pyro/light energy, more footage.
3. — third clip, faster cutting, bolder type hits.
4. — fourth clip, cinematic slow, one big crescendo.
