# 10 Presentation templates
- `DN_Presentation-Template_16x9.pptx` is the master plus a 16-slide sample deck. `.potx` is the same file as a PowerPoint template, and `.pdf` is the rendered check.
- The theme fonts are Newsreader Display (headings) and Public Sans (body). The theme colours are Forest, Ridge, Sage, Gold Text, Sun Gold and Stone; they apply automatically to new charts, tables and shapes.
- **19 layouts:**
  1. Cover: photograph
  2. Cover: engraved plate
  3. Section opener
  4. Executive summary
  5. Title and content
  6. Two columns: strategy
  7. Research finding
  8. Data: full-width chart
  9. Key figures
  10. Timeline
  11. Investment opportunity
  12. Partnership proposal
  13. Financial table
  14. Recommendations
  15. Quotation
  16. Photograph
  17. Closing and contact (real contacts)
  18. Title only
  19. Blank
- **Write the conclusion as the title.** Sample charts and figures are labelled as illustrative where they are not real. The key-figures slide uses three facts sourced to the Constitution.
- Picture placeholders crop to fill. Use photographs from `06/LIBRARY` and caption them in the caption placeholder. Charts are native, so you can right-click and choose Edit Data.
- **Google Slides:** File → Import slides, then add Newsreader and Public Sans through *More fonts*.
