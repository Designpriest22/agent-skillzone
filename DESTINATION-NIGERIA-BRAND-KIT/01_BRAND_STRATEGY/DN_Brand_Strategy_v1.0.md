# Destination Nigeria — Brand Foundation v1.0

> **Source note.** The executive concept note was not among the supplied files. This foundation is built from the project brief's summary of it ("Experience. Invest. Explore." and the platform description) and from the supplied logo artwork. Please check it against the concept note before external use. Where a statement depends on facts that only the client can confirm (partners, mandates, programmes, figures), it is phrased as an aspiration or left as a placeholder.

---

## 1. Brand essence
**Horizons, opened.**
Destination Nigeria opens up the country's landscapes, cultures and opportunities to people who have not yet seen them, and makes the route to each one clear.

## 2. Brand purpose
To grow Nigeria's tourism economy by connecting the people who make experiences (states, communities, operators, creatives) with the people who want to enjoy them or invest in them, and to do it with clear coordination and credible information.

## 3. Positioning
**For** travellers, investors and partners who want to understand Nigeria beyond the headlines,
**Destination Nigeria is** the national tourism development, investment and destination-marketing platform
**that** brings together destinations, institutions, businesses and communities under one trusted, coordinated identity,
**because** it combines the warmth of a destination brand with the rigour of an institution.

## 4. Brand promise
*We will show you Nigeria as it is: varied, creative and full of opportunity. We will give you what you need to experience it, invest in it and explore it with confidence.*

## 5. Brand personality
| Trait | We are | We are not |
|---|---|---|
| Ambitious | Confident about what Nigeria offers; future-facing | Boastful or exaggerated |
| Welcoming | Warm, generous, hospitable in tone | Overfamiliar or slangy in institutional settings |
| Credible | Precise, sourced, organised | Bureaucratic or opaque |
| Curious | Hungry to discover; specific about places and people | Generic ("vibrant Africa") |
| Contemporary | Creative and current; Nigerian design and talent first | Trend-chasing or stereotypical |

## 6. Brand values
1. **Authenticity.** We tell real stories about real places, credit the people behind them, and never fabricate.
2. **Coordination.** One platform and one voice, built in partnership with states, institutions and communities.
3. **Opportunity.** Tourism as an engine for livelihoods, enterprise and investment.
4. **Stewardship.** Responsible tourism that protects heritage, nature and host communities.
5. **Excellence.** International standards in service, information and presentation.

## 7. Audiences
**Primary**
- Domestic travellers and the Nigerian diaspora planning visits home
- International leisure and cultural travellers
- Tourism investors, developers, financiers and development partners
- Federal, state and local tourism institutions and agencies (as collaborators)

**Secondary**
- Tourism operators, hospitality businesses, guides and transport providers
- Host communities, cultural custodians and creative practitioners
- Media, trade press and travel influencers
- Corporate sponsors and event partners

## 8. Messaging hierarchy
1. **Proposition (campaign line):** EXPERIENCE. INVEST. EXPLORE.
2. **Platform statement:** Destination Nigeria is a national platform for tourism development, investment and destination marketing.
3. **Pillars**

| Pillar | For | Core message | Proof points to develop (to be verified) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Experience** | Travellers, diaspora | Nigeria's culture, food, festivals, music and hospitality, met first-hand. | Curated destination features; festival calendar; operator directory |
| **Invest** | Investors, partners | Tourism is an investable, coordinated sector with real projects. | Investment profiles with verified data; partner frameworks |

## 9. Tone of voice
**Clear, warm, assured.** Use short sentences. Use specific nouns: name the place, the dish, the festival, the person. Use active verbs.

| Context | Dial | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign / social | Warmer, more evocative | "Granite giants. Golden mornings. Your next horizon is closer than you think." |
| Destination feature | Descriptive, sensory, factual | "At first light the rock turns from grey to amber. Bring water and good shoes." |
| Investment / institutional | Precise, structured, measured | "This profile summarises the opportunity, its development stage and the documentation available on request." |
| Media | Neutral, attributable | "Destination Nigeria today announced…" (with verified facts only) |

**Do:** write "Nigeria's" not "Africa's"; credit communities and photographers; use numbers only when they are sourced.
**Don't:** use clichés ("hidden gem", "land of contrasts", "vibrant tapestry"); overclaim (the "biggest" or "best" without a source); stereotype; imply endorsements or schedules that have not been confirmed.

**Style points:** British English spelling, as used in Nigerian official writing. Write "Destination Nigeria" in full on first mention; never abbreviate it to "DN" in public copy. Set the campaign line in capitals with full stops: EXPERIENCE. INVEST. EXPLORE.

## 10. Visual identity principles
1. **The logo leads; the landscape speaks.** The supplied logo stays intact. Its inselberg, sun and horizon lines become the system's graphic language.
2. **One sun.** Gold is light, not decoration: one focal gold moment per composition.
3. **Horizon structure.** Layouts are organised around a horizon: a strong horizontal division, generous sky (space), and grounded content.
4. **Two registers, one family.** *Destination* register: cinematic, full-bleed imagery, display serif, evocative. *Institutional* register: ivory or white grounds, structured grid, Inter, restraint. Both registers share the same colours, type, logo and horizon lines.
5. **Authentic Nigeria.** Real places, real people, credited photography. We draw on the country's landforms (granite inselbergs, the Niger–Benue confluence, coastline and savannah), not generic "African" pattern clichés.
6. **Craft over effects.** No drop shadows, bevels, glows, stock gradients or decorative noise. Flat colour, fine lines, considered space.

### Visual territory: "Horizon & Inselberg"
- **Inselberg.** Nigeria's granite monoliths rise abruptly from the plain. The logo's mountain evokes that silhouette. In the system it is a symbol of arrival and landmark.
- **Sun disc.** The gold sun is warmth, hospitality and opportunity. It serves as focal point, marker and bullet.
- **Horizon lines.** The tapering swashes beneath the mountain become contour lines, dividers, route lines and photo masks.
- **Confluence curve.** The flowing tail of the "D" reads as a river. It recalls the meeting of the Niger and the Benue, the river that gives the country its name. In the system it is used for connection, partnership and route graphics.

Cultural references beyond these landforms (textiles such as *aso-oke* or *adire*, architecture, scripts) should only be added in collaboration with the communities and practitioners they belong to, with credit. Don't use them as generic ornament.
