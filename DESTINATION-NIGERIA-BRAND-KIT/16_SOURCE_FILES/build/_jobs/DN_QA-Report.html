Manual review (visual, from renders)

Every v2 deliverable was rendered and inspected as an image during the rebuild. These defects were found and fixed: - The engraved plates were stretched about 25 % vertically by a non-uniform resize. The cropping now keeps the photograph's true proportions, and every dependent file was rebuilt. - The Word templates rendered titles in Calibri because theme-font attributes overrode the brand font. Theme attributes are now stripped from styles and from the document defaults. - Social layouts: a sun disc overlapped contact text (1:1), a place title wrapped onto its standfirst (16:9), statement and figure posts were top-heavy, and the contact plate showed sky hairlines behind the logo. A quiet-sky plate was added. - The website hero cropped out the visitor at Agbokim. The crop was rebalanced. - On the plate-paper background, the sun drifted away from the rock after the plate correction. It was repositioned. - In the guidelines, the contents page numbers were wrong, a plate tile overflowed, and the quick reference printed landscape. All three were fixed. - In the film, the photograph is checked to sit exactly on its plate during each dissolve, with frames inspected at 5 fps through both transitions. Cuts land on the downbeats of the score.

Known limitations

  • Photography rights: the supplied images carry no photographer or licence data, and several look like web downloads. They are used as layout material. Each must be cleared before publication.
  • Tarkwa Bay: the location is identified only by the file name, and the turquoise water and white sand are unusual for Lagos. The image is used only on a presentation board. Confirm it before captioning.
  • Low resolution: five supplied images are under 800 px. They are used only at small sizes (grid tiles, thumbnails). In the 9:16 film, the Zuma Rock photograph is enlarged about 1.4×.
  • Website address: destinationnigeria.org is inferred from the email domain. Confirm it before print.
  • Office fonts: Newsreader and Public Sans must be installed, or embedded in the file, for Office documents to render as designed. Without them, Office substitutes Georgia and Arial.
  • Connectors: the Adobe, Canva and Figma connectors could not be reached from this environment (proxy 403). Files are delivered as open formats (SVG, PDF, DOCX, PPTX, HTML) that import into those tools.
