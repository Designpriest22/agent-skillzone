Destination Nigeria — Brand Guidelines v1.0
02 · Brand overview

Horizons, opened.

Destination Nigeria is a national tourism development, investment and destination-marketing platform. It connects public institutions, states, businesses, communities, operators, investors and creative partners. Its identity has two jobs: to make Nigeria feel irresistible to experience, and credible to invest in.

This foundation is based on the project brief. The executive concept note was not supplied with the artwork, so check it against that note before publication.

Essence

Horizons, opened.

Purpose

To grow Nigeria’s tourism economy by connecting the people who make experiences with the people who want to enjoy them or invest in them, through clear coordination and credible information.

Promise

We will show you Nigeria as it is: varied, creative and full of opportunity. We will give you what you need to experience it, invest in it and explore it with confidence.

03 · Purpose & positioning

Two registers, one family

Positioning

For travellers, investors and partners who want to understand Nigeria beyond the headlines, Destination Nigeria is the national platform that brings destinations, institutions, businesses and communities together under one trusted, coordinated identity.

Personality

  • Ambitious, not boastful
  • Welcoming, not over-familiar
  • Credible, not bureaucratic
  • Curious, not generic
  • Contemporary, not trend-chasing

Values

  • Authenticity
  • Coordination
  • Opportunity
  • Stewardship
  • Excellence

Destination register

Cinematic, full-bleed imagery. Display serif. Evocative copy. One sun.

Institutional register

Ivory or white grounds. Structured grid. Inter. Sourced data. Restraint.

07 · Clear space

Clear space = x

x = the height of the lowercase “n” in the wordmark, about 11% of the logo’s width.

Keep at least 1x clear on all sides of the primary, horizontal and stacked logos. Keep 0.25 × the diameter clear around the Horizon Roundel.

No text, image edge, other logo or graphic device may enter this zone. Allow more space where you can.

18 · Photography direction

Real places, real people, warm light

Principles

  • Specific: a named place, dish, festival or maker, never a generic “Africa” shot.
  • Human: people as protagonists with agency, photographed with consent.
  • Golden-hour warmth: creamy highlights and gentle shadows lifted toward Forest.
  • Horizon composition: leave sky or calm space for type.
  • Dignified: no poverty tourism, no exoticising and no staged “tribal” clichés.

Placeholder art direction: horizon low, sun as focal point, space for type. Replace with licensed photography.

Grading

  • Shadows +5–10% toward Forest #103428.
  • Keep skin tones true; never grade skin green or grey.
  • Text overlays use the Forest gradient (05/11), never black.
  • Duotone (Forest → Savannah) is for texture only, never for portraits.
  • Avoid HDR, heavy teal-and-orange and over-saturated greens.

19 · Image licensing & attribution

Every image needs a record

No photograph in this kit shows a real place. Every image slot holds a clearly labelled, illustrated placeholder.

Stock libraries searched during production did not return verifiable Nigerian locations. Using foreign landscapes would misrepresent the country, so none were used.

Before publishing any image

  • Record the source, photographer, licence, licence ID, location (verified), date and model/property releases in 06/SOURCE_RECORDS/image-register.csv.
  • Credit format: Photo: Name / Agency, in the caption or the credits list.
  • Use AI-generated imagery only if it is labelled as such, and never to depict real destinations, people or events.
  • Get written consent from identifiable people, and community approval for sacred or ceremonial subjects.

22 · Layout, grid & spacing

Horizon structure

  • Grid: 12 columns. A4 margins 22 mm; 16:9 slides 0.75 in; web max 1240 px with 24 px gutters.

Sonic logo · 6.5 s

D major pentatonic. A dùndún-style talking-drum phrase “speaks”, then a lamellophone ascent rises to the sun moment. Synced to motion A.

Sting · 2.5 s

Drum glide, three tines, bell. Used for web welcomes, short social and bumpers (motion B and E).

Intro bed · 30 s loop

A 12/8 groove with bell pattern, shekere, bass and tines. Seamless loop at −18 LUFS. The masters are −16 LUFS, 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV, plus 320 kbps MP3.

The audio is an original composition, fully synthesised in code (16_SOURCE_FILES/build/sonic.py). It uses no samples and no third-party recordings. Recording live instruments with Nigerian musicians is recommended for v2.

28 · Accessibility

Designed for everyone

Colour & type

  • Body contrast ≥ 4.5:1; UI ≥ 3:1.
  • Body text ≥ 10 pt in print, 16 px on the web.
  • No gold text on light grounds.

Digital

  • Visible focus: 3 px Sun Gold ring.
  • Alt text for every image, and captions for all video.
  • Honour reduced motion (the loader and cards do).
  • Error messages in text, not colour alone.

Documents & events

  • Use real Word heading styles, so PDFs are tagged.
  • Large-print versions on request.
  • Signage: 40 mm letters for 10 m viewing, and pictograms with text.
  • Plain language first.

32 · Governance

Brand asset governance

Ownership

The brand owner, to be named by Destination Nigeria, approves new templates, partner co-branding and any change to the masters.

Approval flow

  1. Draft from an official template.
  2. Fact and source check, with the verification line completed.
  3. Brand check against the quick-reference sheet.
  4. Sign-off by the brand owner (external use).

Partners & co-branding

Partner logos appear only with a signed agreement and approved files. The partner badge is for contracted partners only. Never imply government affiliation, endorsement or official status that has not been confirmed in writing.

Version the kit like software. Record every change in 18_QA_AND_HANDOVER/CHANGELOG.md and rebuild with the scripts in 16_SOURCE_FILES/build.

33 · Approved & prohibited applications

Do and don’t

Approved

  • Official templates from this kit
  • The master logo files, unaltered
  • Verified facts with sources
  • Licensed, credited photography
  • Campaign line as a separate lockup
  • Partner badge with a signed agreement

Prohibited

  • Fake or implied official seals, crests or coats of arms
  • Unverified partner, sponsor or government logos
  • Invented statistics, dates, addresses or bank details
  • Stretched, recoloured or retyped logos
  • Generic “African” patterns and stereotypes
  • Political or commercial endorsements without approval
