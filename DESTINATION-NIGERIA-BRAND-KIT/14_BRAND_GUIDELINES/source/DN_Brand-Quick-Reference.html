The essentials on one sheet

Logo

  • Use the master files only (02_LOGO_MASTER_FILES).
  • Clear space: 1 × the height of the “n” on all sides.
  • Minimum: 35 mm / 140 px (small-format: 25 mm / 100 px).
  • Full colour on Ivory or White, reversed on Forest or photography.
  • Never stretch, recolour, rotate, outline, add effects or retype.

Campaign line

Set separately from the logo. Inter SemiBold, caps, +0.22 em, with Sun Gold full stops.

Always

  • Start from an official template.
  • Verify every fact, date, name and figure.
  • Use licensed, credited photography.
  • Leave space: one horizon, one sun.

Never

  • Invent seals, addresses, bank details or statistics.
  • Show partner logos without a signed agreement.
  • Imply that an unconfirmed event is scheduled.
  • Use generic “African” patterns or stereotypes.
