# Archive: v1 motion

**25 s illustrated intro (F), v1.** Kept here because it was commissioned separately, with its illustrated characters: a drummer, dancers, a traveller and a professional. It is superseded by the v2 brand film (`../BRAND_FILM_25s`). It uses v1 devices that the v2 system retires: sun bloom, radiating rings, ribbon wipes, iris transitions, embers, gradient skies, and tracked capitals with gold dots. Don't use it alongside v2 material without re-grading it to the v2 rules.

The v1 logo animations (A cinematic reveal, B minimal reveal, C presentation opener, D brand loop, E symbol) have been removed from the deliverables and replaced by H (logo reveal), I (sting) and J (plate loop). All of them remain in the repository history at kit v1.1.

`SOURCE_PROJECTS/` holds the v1 compositions for reference. They are not wired to the v2 renderer.
