# 02 Logo master files

Every file reuses the supplied vector paths. Nothing has been redrawn. The originals are in `_SUPPLIED_ORIGINALS/` and are untouched.

| # | Variant | File stem | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary, full colour (**master**) | `SVG/DN_logo_primary_fullcolour` | Default on Ivory or White |
| 2 | Horizontal lockup | `SVG/DN_logo_horizontal_fullcolour` | Headers, footers, narrow bands (secondary) |
| 3 | Stacked lockup | `SVG/DN_logo_stacked_fullcolour` | Square and portrait spaces (secondary) |
| 4 | Compact symbol: Horizon Roundel (forest, ivory, gold) | `SVG/DN_symbol_roundel_*` | Avatars, app icons, favicons |
| 5 | Symbol only: landscape | `SVG/DN_symbol_landscape_fullcolour` | Graphic sign-off, merchandise |
| 6 | One-colour green | `MONOCHROME/*_green-1c` | One-colour print |
| 7 | White reversed (all white) | `REVERSED/*_white` | Photography, video, dark grounds |
| 7b | Reversed (white plus gold sun, as supplied) | `REVERSED/*_reversed` | Forest and Charcoal grounds |
| 8 | Black | `MONOCHROME/*_black-1c` | Fax, newsprint, stamps |
| 9 | Production one-colour compound path | `MONOCHROME/DN_logo_primary_production-1c-compound` | Dies, foil, emboss, laser, vinyl, embroidery |
| 10 | Small-format digital | `SVG/*_small-format`, `REVERSED/*_small-format` | 100–160 px / 25–35 mm |

**Formats:** `SVG/` `REVERSED/` `MONOCHROME/` hold the editable vector sources. `PDF/` holds vector PDFs that open as editable artwork in Illustrator. `EPS/` holds vector EPS converted from those PDFs. `PNG/` holds transparent PNGs from 64 to 4000 px.

**Documented changes:** (1) the reversed masters centre the first “i” dot, correcting the supplied Asset_1. (2) The small-format version drops the hairline secondary horizon and adds a 0.35-unit optical stroke to NIGERIA. (3) The Horizon Roundel and the horizontal and stacked lockups are new secondary arrangements of the original elements.

**Clear space:** 1x on all sides, where x is the height of the lowercase “n” (about 11% of the logo width). **Minimum width:** 35 mm / 140 px for the primary, 25 mm / 100 px for small-format, 8 mm / 16 px for the roundel.
