# 06 Photography and media

- `placeholders/` holds illustrated landscape stand-ins (dawn, day and dusk) at 3:2, 4:5 and 9:16, as SVG plus JPG. Templates, mockups and decks use these until licensed photography is supplied. **They are not photographs of real destinations.**
- `SOURCE_RECORDS/image-register.csv` is the register every published image must have a row in.
- `LICENSES_AND_ATTRIBUTION/LICENSES.md` records the licences for everything in the kit and sets out the photography rules.

**Shot list to commission:** landscapes (inselbergs, waterfalls, coast, savannah, highlands) at golden hour; festivals and cultural practice, with community consent; food and hospitality; makers and creative industries; transport and arrival; investment sites, including drone and context shots; and portraits of operators and hosts. Frame wide with a low horizon to leave space for type.
