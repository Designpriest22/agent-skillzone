## Photography
No stock photography is included. The only photographs are the client-supplied images. Before you publish any photograph:
1. Record it in `../SOURCE_RECORDS/image-register.csv`.
2. Keep the licence document or invoice in this folder (`LICENSES_AND_ATTRIBUTION/[asset_id]_licence.pdf`).
3. Credit it as: *Photo: Name / Agency*.
4. Obtain model releases, property releases and community consent where they apply.
5. Do not use AI-generated imagery to depict real places, people or events. If you use it for abstract texture, label it as AI-generated.
