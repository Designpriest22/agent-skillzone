Meat & Greet

Since 2020 · Free BBQ Party

Meat & Greet is not just a barbecue event — it's a culture-driven social experience that brings together food, music, entertainment, networking, lifestyle, and community into one destination experience. Founded in Abuja, it has grown into one of the city's most recognizable lifestyle event brands, creating an environment where strangers become friends, creatives meet collaborators, brands meet consumers, and communities gather around great food and unforgettable experiences. The event has successfully delivered over 16 editions and continues to evolve into a larger cultural movement. As the brand puts it: "People come for the meat, but stay for the experience."

Venue

Outdoor venues across Abuja, FCT

Pillars

Food, Music, Social Connection, Entertainment, Lifestyle.

Festival of Lights

Since 2018 · Created by Sabigroove & IBlend

Festival of Lights is one of Abuja's premier lifestyle and entertainment festivals, celebrated for its fusion of music, culture, fashion, food, art, and immersive live experiences. Created to showcase the vibrant spirit of Nigeria's capital, it has become a signature annual gathering where thousands of attendees come together for world-class performances, premium production, and unforgettable cultural moments — renowned for its large-scale visual installations, high-energy performances, and interactive brand activations.

Festival of Lights was brought to life by Sabigroove and IBlend, whose vision established it as one of Abuja's most recognizable cultural properties. TCM serves as a strategic partner in its creation and execution, contributing creative strategy, production coordination, marketing support, community engagement, talent coordination, and experience design — including TCM's own Guinness Bright House activation within the festival.

Founding Partners

Sabigroove — Creator & Entertainment Platform · IBlend — Creative & Experience Partner

Venue

Exhibition Pavilion, Area 11, Garki, Abuja, FCT

Romie's Block

Abuja's Biggest Monday Night

In most cities, Monday is the night nobody fights for. In Abuja, DJ Romie turned it into the highlight of the week. Romie's Block is the weekly Monday night event hosted by DJ Romie at Papiees Meatro, Jahi — moving through Afrobeats anthems, Amapiano's rolling basslines, and Afro-House grooves for a crowd of Abuja's young professionals, creatives, and nightlife regulars who come back week after week. Read the full feature in DJ Romie's profile.

Venue

Papiees Meatro, Jahi, Abuja

FAQ
