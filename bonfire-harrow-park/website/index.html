Site layout · 4 areas

Zones at a glance

Harrow Park splits into four areas. Guests arrive from Ahmadu Bello Way, pass the entry checks, and walk into the general space with the stage at its south end.

Arrival

Road access, parking and entry control.

  • Ahmadu Bello WayMain road running past the park; all arrivals come in from here.
  • Entrance & validation pointsTwo entry check points on the access road before the park.
  • BarricadesPerimeter and crowd-control lines along the entry route.
  • ParkingMain parking lot to the north, separate VIP parking by the entry road.

Audience

Where guests spend the night.

  • General spaceThe main lawn in front of the stage, from the barrier back past the delay towers.
  • VIP tablesTable zones on both flanks of the stage with a side-on view.
  • Fun experience areaActivation space to the north-east of the lawn.
  • Experience areaSecond activation zone beside the parking lot.

Services

Food, drink and facilities.

  • VendorsFood and drink row along the north-east edge.
  • Sales point & POSBy the main building, near the entrance.
  • ToiletsToilet block on the north side.
  • Keep clearThe pool and the sand bunkers on the lawn stay out of bounds.

Production

Stage, power and control. Crew only.

  • Main stage & runwaySouth end of the general space, facing north.
  • Backstage & green roomsIn the compound behind the stage.
  • Generators2 × 250 kVA, behind the stage.
  • FOH & delay towersFOH at ~30 m, two delay towers with screens at 40 m.

Production sheet · Rev A

The stage

Dimensions from the stage and site drawings (ABJ-A-01 to 04). The stage sits at the south end of the general space, facing north towards the main building.

Stage deck
56 × 40 ft

Deck height 1.8 m, with a 32 × 16 ft runway out into the crowd.

Roof
60 × 40 × 40 ft

Ground-support truss system, 18.3 m wide and 12.2 m tall.

Main LED wall
16 × 7 m

Centre screen behind the band, bottom edge 3 m above deck.

Live-feed screens
2 × 5.5 × 7.5 m

Camera feed either side of the stage, so the back of the lawn sees the faces.

Main PA
12 + 8 per side

YME GT13 line arrays (main and outfill hangs), plus 20 subs across the front.

Delay towers
2 @ 40 m

Speakers and 3 × 5 m screens at 40 m out, so sound and picture reach the back.

Front of house
6 × 6 m

Three-level control tower, about 30 m from the stage.

Crowd barrier
3 m pit

Front-of-stage barrier with a 3 m working pit for security and cameras.

Mid truss
9.9 m

Spark curtain and moving lights flown over the deck.
