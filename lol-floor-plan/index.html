Notes for brands

  • Sizes are width × height. Every wall is 10 ft (3.05 m) high. Send print-ready vector artwork (PDF/AI) at the exact slot size, named with your slot code.
  • Eagle Square is a concrete parade ground. Nothing can be staked, so all frames stand on weighted bases.
  • December is Harmattan season (dry, dusty, gusty). Long walls use wind-permeable mesh. The show runs after dark, so light your wall if you want it seen at night.
  • Pink lines are barricades. Do not fix branding to them without production approval.
  • Plans are not to scale. Production confirms final dimensions on site.
