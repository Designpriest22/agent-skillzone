Sheet 06 · Artwork & build guide

Building on Eagle Square

Event13 Dec 2026, gates 16:00HeadlineWizkid, 22:00

Artwork

  • Supply artwork at the exact slot size: width × 10 ft high.
  • Vector PDF or AI with fonts outlined, CMYK. Raster images at 100 dpi at full size.
  • Keep logos and copy inside a 6 in (150 mm) safe margin. Add 2 in (50 mm) bleed.
  • Keep key messages above 3 ft from the ground. Crowds block the bottom of each wall.
  • Name files with your slot code, e.g. T03_BrandName_50x10.pdf.

The venue

  • Eagle Square is a concrete parade ground of about 3 ha with about 2 ha of parking, on Shehu Shagari Way in the Central Business District.
  • The surface cannot take stakes or anchors. Every frame stands on ballast or weighted bases.
  • December is Harmattan season: dry, dusty and gusty. Use wind-permeable mesh on long walls and wind-rated frames.
  • The event runs after dark, so light or backlight any wall you want seen after 18:30.

On site

  • Every pink line on the plans is a barricade line. Do not move, lean on or fix branding to barricades without production sign-off.
  • Walls stay inside the slot's footprint. Nothing projects into a walk lane, corridor or crash-barrier zone.
  • Activations in the Brands Experience Zone need a separate layout approval: power, staff numbers and footprint depth.
  • Build and strike times are issued by production after slots are confirmed.
Before you print, confirm with production: (1) gate and road names around the site; (2) footprint depth behind each wall and in the Brands Experience Zone; (3) the 80 × 10 ft slots V18–V19, which are drawn shorter than their stated size on the supplied plan; (4) turnstile count (the validation drawing shows 7). All sizes come from the organiser's floor plans. The plans are diagrammatic, so a site survey confirms the final dimensions.
