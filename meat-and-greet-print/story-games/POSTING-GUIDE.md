FirstKlaz × Meat & Greet · Sat 31 Oct · Doxa Park, Abuja · 5PM · 18+ · tickets: pv.rsvp/firstklaz-meating

**Files**
- `frames/`: the 21 clean stories, 1080×1920. Post these.
- `guide/`: the same stories with a dashed box showing where each sticker goes and what to type in it. Reference only; don't post these.
