**Abuja context (general knowledge, not artist-specific):** Abuja was chosen in 1976 as the new federal capital, partly for its central, ethnically neutral location. It became the seat of government in 1991. "Wazobia" combines the word for "come" in Yoruba (*wá*), Hausa (*zo*) and Igbo (*bia*).

**Not claimed anywhere in the copy:** past Meat & Greet appearances, his performance history in Abuja, awards, streaming numbers, or any direct quote. If the team has verified quotes or footage from an Abuja show, the copy can get stronger with them.

## 2. The narrative

**The insight:** FirstKlaz's sound is Fuji from the South-West, Arewa from the North, and Afrobeats over both. That is what Abuja was built to be: a capital at the centre of the country, where Nigeria meets. His remix title says it in one word: *Arewazobia*.

**Campaign line (kept from the brief, because the research supports it):**
**ABUJA DIDN'T JUST HEAR THE SOUND. IT HELPED SHAPE IT.**

**Recurring device: "where Nigeria meets"**
- **Mark:** a meeting-point crosshair stamped with Abuja's coordinates (9.0765° N, 7.3986° E).
- **Running chapter counter:** each slide carries 01/08 to 08/08, like a track list.
- **Pay-off:** slide 6 reveals *Wa. Zo. Bia.* Slide 8 turns that "come" into the invitation.

**Story arc:**
1. The claim.
2. The city.
3. The boy.
4. The sound.
5. The records.
6. The city again, now in the story.
7. The night.
8. The call.

## 3. Copy deck

Headline / body / meta. Body copy is capped at about 45 words per slide for mobile.

### 01 — The statement
- **Kicker:** MEAT & GREET PRESENTS · FIRSTKLAZ
- **Headline:** ABUJA DIDN'T JUST HEAR THE SOUND. IT HELPED SHAPE IT.
- **Body:** He grew up in a city where North and South share the same streets. Then he put both into his music. On 31 October, FirstKlaz brings it home.
- **Meta:** 01/08 · Swipe →
- **Visual:** a tight, cinematic hero portrait (real photo), warm amber duotone. The headline sits in cream across the lower third. Meat & Greet logo top-left. Meeting-point mark top-right.

### 02 — The city
- **Headline:** BUILT WHERE NIGERIA MEETS.
- **Body:** Abuja was planned at the centre of the country, a capital meant to belong to all of it. Grow up here and your soundtrack is never one language. Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo in the same market. Afrobeats over all of it.
- **Meta:** 02/08 · 9.0765° N, 7.3986° E
- **Visual:** real Abuja street-life photography: Wuse Market energy, an okada or keke at dusk, young people outside a spot. No skyline stock. Archive border inspired by Northern geometric embroidery.

### 03 — Before the name travelled
- **Headline:** BEFORE THE NAME TRAVELLED, THE SOUND WAS TAKING SHAPE.
- **Body:** Roots in Nasarawa, raised in Abuja. By his own account: church choir, his father's R&B, rock and reggae on repeat, and a school nickname, "first class", that he decided to keep. Then years of putting singles out himself.
- **Meta:** 03/08 · FROM THE ARCHIVE
- **Visual:** an early-career photo, only if the artist's team supplies an authentic one. Shown as a contact sheet with sprocket holes and grain. If none exists, use a type-led layout. Never a fake archive photo.

### 04 — The sound
- **Headline:** FUJI IN ONE HAND. AREWA IN THE OTHER.
- **Body:** Take sounds Nigeria's elders built, then flip them for a Gen-Z crowd. Fuji grit. Northern melodies and talking drums. Afrobeats bounce underneath. Old school carried by new-school melodies, he says. That's why every title starts with Gen-Z.
- **Meta:** 04/08 · OLD RECORDS / NEW BLOOD
- **Visual:** a live performance shot, mid-motion. Split-tone in two halves: amber (Fuji, South-West) and earth-red (Arewa, North), joined by the crosshair.

### 05 — The records
- **Headline:** FOUR RECORDS. ONE IDENTITY.

  - **04 GEN-Z AREWA** · with Iliya Entertainment Music: a veteran Northern band on a Gen-Z record. The one that made the idea impossible to ignore.
- **Footer line:** Then came *Dejavu*, and in 2026, *Tsammani* and *Salama*. The story kept moving.

## 4. Design system

- **Palette:**
  - Warm charcoal #17120F (base)
  - Roasted earth #4A2A1C (panels)
  - Burnt amber #C8641E (accent, Fuji)
  - Cream #EFE6D6 (type)
  - Meat & Greet blood red #B3121C (only on slides 7–8 and the CTA)
  - Ember #E0592A (small highlights)
- **Type:**
  - Anton (condensed display; also the Meat & Greet headline face) for headlines.
  - Archivo for body text at 34–40 px minimum.
  - IBM Plex Mono for archive metadata (counters, coordinates, dates).
- **Grid:** 72 px outer margin.
  - Logo top-left at 150 px wide.
  - Chapter counter and crosshair top-right.
  - Headline zone in the lower 45%.
  - Body copy in a max 8-column measure.
- **Image treatment:** warm duotone (charcoal to amber), fine film grain, slight archive wear on the edges. Photos sit in contact-sheet or torn-paper frames on slides 3 and 5.
- **Motifs:**
  - Abuja crosshair with coordinates.
  - A Northern-embroidery-inspired geometric border, drawn as an original pattern (not a copy of a specific garment or building).
  - Grill-mark diagonal rules linking back to the Meat & Greet steak icon.
- **Logo:** the supplied Meat & Greet logo on every slide (small), large on slide 8. No FirstKlaz logo unless his team supplies one.
- **Originality vs the reference:** no medals, no camouflage, no star clusters. The identity rests on the crosshair, the chapter counter, the embroidery border and the duotone.
