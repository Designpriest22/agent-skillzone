The Movement

Our Story →
2016

TCM Festival launches — running ever since

TCM owned. TCM is founded in Abuja, and TCM Festival becomes the first project to carry the name — a TCM-owned property that's run every year since.

2018

Capital Block Party, Festival of Lights

Both partnerships. Capital Block Party and Festival of Lights launch the same year, establishing TCM as a mover of crowds and a partner brands trust with their biggest nights.

2020

Meat & Greet launches

TCM owned. Meat & Greet launches as a TCM-owned property — a free BBQ party that becomes a signature fixture on the calendar.

2024

Brands start moving with TCM

Guinness, Heineken, Pepsi, Hennessy, and more move alongside TCM festivals as partners, embedding brands inside the culture itself.

NOW

Capital Machine, Culture Fest

TCM moves owned festivals, partner productions, talent, and brand campaigns at full scale — Abuja's culture engine, fully online.
