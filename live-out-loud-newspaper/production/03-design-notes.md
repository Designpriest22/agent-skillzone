# 03 · Art direction and brand check: Graphic Design (Ife)

- **System:** vintage broadsheet on warm newsprint (`#EFE8D9`) with paper grain and vignette. Ink is `#1A1613`, and there is one spot colour, LOL orange (`#D9481A`), taken from the flyer's zig-zag.
- **Type (two families):**
  - Playfair Display for masthead, headlines and pull quotes.
  - Old Standard TT for body text, labels and the puzzle grid.
- **Photos:** Wizkid images from the client's PDF, plus `assets/talent/wizkid.jpg`. All are rendered as greyscale halftone so the paper reads as one printed object. The template's stock filler was removed: house scaffolding, food truck, fairground ride.
- **Sponsors:**
  - The 13 logos are cropped as one strip from the official flyer at full resolution.
  - The flyer's textured background is flattened to solid ink so the logos sit in a printed black band.
  - No logo was redrawn, recoloured or reordered.
  - The band is on page 3 ("Our Partners").
- **Puzzle:** a 13 × 13 grid hides 19 words in all 8 directions. The 33 unused cells spell **GET YOUR TICKET NOW AT WIZKID TIX AFRICA**. The generator (`tools/wordsearch.py`) checks every word and the message against the grid before writing it.
- **Format:** 4 pages at 1080 × 1350 (IG 4:5), plus a print PDF.

**Fixed in review:**
- The masthead and headline wrapped and pushed the caption under the CTA.
- The page 2 banner cropped Wizkid's face, then covered the classifieds.
- A PSA list item broke around the link.
- The page 3 stage photo was too dark, so it was swapped for a portrait.
- The cover had an empty band, now filled with the Ticket Office box and the "In this issue" index.
