More than music

Festivals are no longer only about the music. They are where fashion, photography, art, food and youth culture meet. Live Out Loud is built for exactly that: a night to dress up, show up and be part of the story, in the heart of the capital.

THE GLOBAL RISE
OF AFROBEATS

Afrobeats has grown from a regional sound into one of the world's most celebrated music movements, carried by streaming, international collaborations and sold-out arenas. Wizkid has played a major part in that rise, proving that African music can travel without losing itself, and opening doors for a new generation of Nigerian artists.

Wizkid: from Lagos to the world, and now to Eagle Square.
