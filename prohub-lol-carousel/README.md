# Prohub × Live Out Loud: portfolio carousel

8 slides, 1080×1350 (4:5). The work is laid out on one continuous concrete surface (Eagle Square), so the carousel reads as a single swipe. There is no copy beyond the Prohub logo and one line.

| Slide | Shows | File |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wizkid walk-out footage on a tablet | `slide-1.mp4` |
| 2 | Red feed poster | `slide-2.png` |
| 3 | Red story poster, and the festival reel playing | `slide-3.mp4` |
| 4 | Landscape QR poster, minimal black flyer | `slide-4.png` |
| 5 | Standard Wave 1 sold-out poster, waitlist website | `slide-5.png` |
| 6 | Wizkid stage and pyro footage playing, newspaper front page | `slide-6.mp4` |
| 7 | Word hunt page, Onyx tee | `slide-7.png` |
| 8 | Ember polo, Chalk jersey, Prohub logo | `slide-8.png` |

Video slides are 6 s and carry their source clip's audio. Posting the slides as one mixed-media carousel keeps the set continuous. `out/panorama.jpg` is the whole surface in one image.

Build: `node render.mjs` (or `node render.mjs stills`).
Garments come from `tools_garments.mjs`. Source clips are `assets/heis/C3.mp4` (The Big Wiz Moment) and `assets/heis/REEL.mp4`.
