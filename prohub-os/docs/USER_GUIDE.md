# User guide

## Giving instructions
Open the **Command centre** and type what you want, the way you would brief a senior manager. Include the quantity, the audience, the deadline and the result you want.

Good examples:
- *Find 30 hospitality businesses in Lagos and Abuja with weak social media and add them to the CRM with a recommended approach.*
- *Develop a campaign to acquire 20 new corporate clients this month: research, target list, offer, landing page, content and outreach.*
- *Prepare a proposal for Lekki Suites Hotel for a 3-month social media retainer.*
- *Show me our revenue for October.* · *Which agent has the most outstanding tasks?* · *Prepare a report on our business performance this week.*

The Managing Director either answers straight from live data or plans the work and delegates it. The **Execution** panel shows each delegated task, which agent owns it, its status and its outcome, and it updates live. When everything finishes, you get a consolidated report.

To bypass the Managing Director, choose an agent in **Route to**, or use **Assign a task directly** on that agent's page.

## What the statuses mean
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| proposed | An agent suggested the work; it is waiting for the Operations Manager or you to approve it |
| approved / queued | Accepted and waiting to run (possibly waiting for prerequisite tasks) |
| in progress | An agent is working on it |
| awaiting subtasks | A manager is waiting for the work it delegated |
| completed | Finished, and the records it produced were **verified** to exist |
| failed | Could not finish. The error explains why; you can retry it |

## Approvals
Nothing reaches a client, a prospect or the public without your approval. **Approvals** shows exactly what will be sent or published: the recipient, subject, full message, proposal line items, caption, or a page preview. Approve it and the system carries it out and logs the result. Reject it, optionally with a note, and the draft goes back to editable.

## CRM
- **Pipeline:** move leads between stages. Marking a lead *Won* creates the client.
- **Lead page:** shows verified facts and assumptions side by side, sources, the score breakdown, full contact history and related work. **Start pitch workflow** runs the full research → concept → personalised pitch → approval sequence for that lead.
- **Follow-ups:** due follow-ups are assigned to Client Management automatically.

## Deliverables
Every piece of agent work (reports, scripts, storyboards, copy, designs, plans) opens as a deliverable with its quality-control checklist. **Approve** it, or **Request changes** with notes; requesting changes creates a revision task automatically. Track client sign-off with the client-approval buttons.

## Reports
The **Reports centre** keeps every scheduled and on-demand report. Download any of them as PDF, Excel or CSV. The executive briefing covers: what happened yesterday, what was completed today, opportunities, new leads, sales, revenue received, pending payments, operational problems, recommended actions, and priorities for the next 24 hours.

## Reading the numbers
- **Revenue** is always recorded money received.
- **Pipeline values** are estimates, weighted by stage probability, and are shown separately from revenue.
- **Social metrics** appear only when they come from a connected platform or are entered manually with a source.
