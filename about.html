We Are
The Capital Movement.

TCM is more than an entertainment company — we are Abuja's culture platform, connecting music, nightlife, fashion, live experiences, talent development, brand partnerships, media, and creative services into one movement.

Our Story

Since 2016

TCM was founded in Abuja in 2016 with one festival and one belief: that the capital's creative energy deserved a platform built to match it. What started with TCM Festival has grown into a full culture engine — owned properties like Meat & Greet and OTB by TCM, partnerships with Guinness, Heineken, Pepsi, Hennessy and more, and a talent network spanning the biggest names in Afrobeats and beyond. A decade in, TCM moves events, talent, production, PR & marketing, and community at a scale no one else in Abuja operates at.

Mission

Building the creative economy of Abuja

To build and amplify the creative economy of Abuja through world-class events, talent development, storytelling, and strategic partnerships.

Vision

Abuja recognised as a global culture capital

A future where Abuja stands alongside the world's great culture cities — where its artists, creators, and nightlife set the pace for the continent.
