# Week 01 — Expected Outcomes & Measurement Framework

**Rule:** we measure business outcomes first. Reach and likes are leading indicators only. Every number comes from platform Insights or the lead tracker, never from estimates.

## 1. The scorecard (in order of importance)

| Tier | Metric | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Revenue** | Won deals / ₦ value | Signed and paid (ledger only, never pipeline) | Prohub OS finance ledger |
| **Pipeline** | Proposals sent | Approved proposals sent to WK01 leads | CRM |
| | Brand Direction Calls held | Calls that actually happened | Calendar / CRM |
| **Leads** | Qualified leads (score 8–10) | See qualification rubric | `lead-tracker.csv` |
| | Total conversations | Unique people who DM'd, WhatsApp'd or commented with a business need | `lead-tracker.csv` |
| | Enquiries per post | Conversations tagged to each day's keyword | `lead-tracker.csv` → `source_post_tag` |
| **Attention quality** | Sends per reach (IG) | Shares ÷ accounts reached | IG Insights |
| | Saves per reach (IG) | Saves ÷ accounts reached | IG Insights |
| | Reel 3-sec hold / avg watch time | Hook strength | IG/FB/TikTok Insights |
| | LinkedIn engagement rate | (reactions+comments+reposts+clicks) ÷ impressions; benchmark median 5.72% (Oktopost 2026) | LinkedIn analytics |
| | Profile visits → link taps | Bio/WhatsApp link clicks | IG Insights |
| **Reach** | Accounts reached, non-follower % | Top-of-funnel only | Insights |

## 2. Week-1 targets: set as *floors to beat*, not forecasts

There's no baseline (no historical account data is on file), so week 1 **establishes** it. MD-set minimums for calling the week a success:

| Outcome | Minimum | Stretch |
|---|---|---|
| Conversations with a business need | 25 | 50 |
| Qualified leads (8–10) | 6 | 12 |
| Brand Direction Calls booked | 4 | 8 |
| Proposals sent (owner-approved) | 2 | 4 |
| Day 6 Brand Read handles | 30 | 75 |
| IG sends per reach on Day 2 carousel | > 1% | > 2% |
| LinkedIn engagement rate (documents) | ≥ 5.7% | ≥ 10% |

*These are judgement calls for a new campaign. Week 2 targets will be reset from actual week-1 results.*

## 4. Tracking rhythm
- **Daily (21:30):** Social Media Manager updates `performance-log.csv` (one row per post per platform) and `lead-tracker.csv`.
- **Mon 16 Nov:** Week-1 review by the MD. What to keep, kill and double down on, plus the Brand Reads published.
- **Mon 23 Nov:** Lagging check. Calls held, proposals, any revenue (ledger).

## 5. How findings will change week 2 (decision rules)
- **Format that produced the most *qualified* leads per 1,000 reach** gets 2 slots next week.
- **Any post under 50% of the week's median reach *and* zero conversations**: retire the format or rewrite the hook.
- **Day 5 slide-number DMs**: the most-requested service becomes week 2's showcase.
- **Day 6 question-box answers**: the most common pain point becomes a week 2 carousel.
- **Reel average watch time under 40% of length**: shorten to 10–12 s and front-load the payoff.
- If **LinkedIn** produces the highest proposal value per lead, move one Instagram slot's production effort to LinkedIn.
