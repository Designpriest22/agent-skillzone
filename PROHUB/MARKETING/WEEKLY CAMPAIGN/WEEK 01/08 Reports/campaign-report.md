## 1. The campaign in one paragraph
Nigerian customers aren't short of options. They're short of reasons to trust one: 84% of adults surveyed met a scam last year (GASA 2026). So they choose the business that's *obviously* credible, *obviously* right for them, and *obviously* memorable. **"Make it obvious."** positions Prohub as the team that makes the right choice obvious. Over seven days it goes from a test people can run on their own brand (Day 1) to a time-bound offer for the ₦396bn December season (Day 7). Every post has a single, trackable keyword CTA, so we'll know which post produced which enquiry.

## 2. What was researched
Full summary with sources: `01 Research/research-summary.md`
- **Platforms:** Facebook ≈ 48M, LinkedIn ≈ 14M, Instagram ≈ 9.6M, TikTok ≈ 37M Nigerian users. WhatsApp is where enquiries close.
- **Algorithms:** Instagram ranks on watch time, **sends** and likes per reach. LinkedIn B2B median engagement is 5.72%, and the top performers post more carousels.
- **Buyer problems:** SMEs struggle to build a distinctive identity (2025 studies). Inflation is ~15.9% and the policy rate 26.5%, so the argument has to be about wasted spend.
- **Trust:** 84% scam exposure. Looking credible is a conversion issue.
- **Season:** ₦396.54bn Lagos Detty December 2025 spend. Detty December Fest starts Fri 18 Dec 2026.
- **Competitors:** directory-listed Lagos/Abuja agencies mostly position on service lists. Room to win by **showing judgement in public**.
- **Five opportunities:** Trust Tax · December readiness · send-worthy diagnostics · show the thinking · distinctiveness over decoration.
