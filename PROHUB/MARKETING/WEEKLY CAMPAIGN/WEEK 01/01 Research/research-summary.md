## 2. Key findings

### 2.1 Audience & platforms
- **Facebook is still the biggest reach channel; LinkedIn is bigger than Instagram among Nigerian adults.** NapoleonCat (June 2026): Facebook ≈ 48.4M users, **LinkedIn ≈ 14.0M (5.8% of population)**, Instagram ≈ 9.6M. The 25–34 group is the largest on both Instagram (42.7%) and LinkedIn (7.7M). *Caveat: NapoleonCat pages are internally inconsistent across months. Treat these as directional.*
- **TikTok ≈ 37.4M users (15.8% of the population)**, DataReportal Jan 2025 via Intelpoint. No 2026 figure was found.
- **WhatsApp is where the conversation closes.** User estimates for Nigeria range from 51M to 90–100M depending on source. Click-to-WhatsApp ads (Meta's Messages objective) send ad traffic straight into a chat. Vendor benchmarks claim higher response rates than web forms, but these are vendor claims, not independent data.
- **Cost change to plan for:** Meta is reported to charge Nigerian businesses on the **WhatsApp Business Platform** roughly **₦14 per message from 1 October 2026** (Arise TV, Legit.ng, MSME Africa). A normal WhatsApp Business app number isn't affected the same way. Prohub should confirm which one it uses before scaling WhatsApp follow-ups.

**Implication:** Instagram and Facebook build awareness and the creative showcase. LinkedIn is the high-value channel for corporate clients and marketing managers, and is under-used by Nigerian agencies. WhatsApp is where enquiries are converted, so every CTA should end in a DM or a WhatsApp chat, not a web form.

### 2.2 What the platforms reward
- **Instagram:** Adam Mosseri publicly confirmed (Jan 2025) that the main ranking signals are **watch time, sends per reach (DM shares) and likes per reach**. Several 2026 marketing guides rank sends as the strongest signal for reaching non-followers. The often-quoted "3–5× more weight than likes" figure couldn't be confirmed from Instagram itself.
- **LinkedIn:** Oktopost's March 2026 benchmark of 1,000+ B2B company pages puts the **median engagement rate at 5.72%**, and the top 10% reach 22.45% while posting noticeably more carousels. Carousel benchmarks vary roughly fourfold between sources because each defines "engagement" differently. They tend to win on engagement rate, not on reach.

**Implication:** Design posts to be **sent** ("send this to whoever runs your brand"). Open every Reel with a 1–2 second hook. Publish carousels as **PDF document posts** on LinkedIn.

### 2.3 Problems business owners are trying to solve
- **Weak, inconsistent brand identity is a known constraint for Nigerian SMEs.** A Feb 2025 South-East Nigeria study found many SMEs "find it difficult to develop and maintain a distinctive brand identity" because of limited resources and branding knowledge, and that **digital branding and customer engagement were the strongest predictors of sales performance**. An Aug 2025 Sub-Saharan review lists skills gaps, finance and infrastructure as the main obstacles.
- **Budgets are under pressure.** Headline inflation ≈ **15.9%** (May–June 2026, NBS via Nairametrics/PwC), the CBN policy rate is **26.5%**, and the naira is around ₦1,380/$. Cost of sales and opex at listed companies rose 16.9% in H1 2026. *Implication: talk about wasted spend ("boosting without positioning") and return, not "investing in your brand journey".*
- **Trust is the real conversion barrier.** GASA's *State of Scams in Nigeria 2026* (survey of **1,180 Nigerian adults**) found **84% encountered a scam in the past year** and 22% lost money or data. Older KnowBe4 data (2023) ranked social media as the top scam channel in Nigeria. *Implication: looking credible and consistent is a sales function, not decoration. This is Prohub's sharpest commercial argument.*

### 2.4 Seasonal opportunity — December
- **Lagos Detty December 2025: ₦396.54bn in consumer spending**, with diaspora visitors contributing 55% (MO Africa, *The Economics of Euphoria: Lagos' Detty December 2025*, reported by Nairametrics and BusinessDay). About 3.6M participants over a 55-day season running from mid-November.
- **2026 calendar:** Detty December Fest runs 18–30 Dec 2026 (Wizkid opening night on 18 Dec). Flytime Fest is 22–25 Dec. More than 150 events are expected across Lagos and Accra.
- **Implication:** Event organisers, hospitality, restaurants, entertainment and retail brands are **buying creative now**. The campaign week (9–15 Nov) sits exactly 5–6 weeks before peak, which is the natural lead time for identity refreshes, event branding, promo video and social content. This becomes the urgency for Day 7.

### 2.5 Brand science we can use
- **Distinctive brand assets** (Ehrenberg-Bass Institute): non-name elements such as colour, shape, sound and characters that trigger the brand in buyers' memory. Their value comes from **consistency over time**.
- **Ipsos × JKR:** only **15% of brand assets are "truly distinctive"** (reported by Marketing Week).
- **Implication:** This supports Day 1 ("Cover your logo") with real science and gives the LinkedIn version authority without hype.

### 2.6 What successful agencies post (and what generates enquiries)
- Agency-marketing guides agree on the mix: **process / behind-the-scenes, case studies ("proof beats promises"), tips, and interactive posts**. None of the sources publish enquiry data tying a format to new clients, so **Prohub should measure this itself** (see the Measurement Framework).
- Proof-led formats such as before/after, case studies and teardowns are framed as the strongest lead drivers, but Prohub has **no client case studies cleared for publication on file**. *Strategic response:* a clearly labelled **fictional rebrand** (Day 3) shows the full process without borrowing anyone's work or making claims we can't back up.

## 3. Competitor positioning (category scan)

**Who shows up for Lagos/Abuja branding searches** (directory listings, not an endorsement): Brand Envoy Africa, Nelson Reids, Cybertron Ads, DBO Agency (Abuja and Lagos, founded 2016), TMA Brand Consulting, BeeTcore, Feman Media Agency, Hans Finest Digital Solutions, Mediatransit (Clutch Lagos, updated July 2026). Abuja names include Raven & Macaw and Kenneth Price Nigeria (TechBehemoths lists 31 branding companies in Abuja).

**Category pattern** (*strategic rationale*, based on directory self-descriptions plus the content-mix guidance above):
1. **Service-list positioning:** "full-service / 360° / results-driven / we offer branding, web, digital marketing…". It's interchangeable, which is exactly the "you look like everyone you compete with" problem we tell clients about on Day 2.
2. **Portfolio posts without the thinking:** finished logos and flyers with little explanation of the commercial reason behind them.
3. **Directory ratings rest on very few reviews** (e.g. 6 reviews behind a 4.8★), and many listings are paid placements. Buyers can't easily tell agencies apart, which leaves room for whoever **demonstrates judgement in public**.

**Where Prohub can stand apart:**
- Teach the **diagnosis**, not just show the deliverable: positioning, trust, distinctiveness.
- Speak in **Nigerian business owner language** ("Check your DM for price", "Our website is a WhatsApp link") instead of agency jargon.
- **Strategy before spend.** In a 26.5% rate environment, Prohub is the agency that tells you what *not* to spend on.
