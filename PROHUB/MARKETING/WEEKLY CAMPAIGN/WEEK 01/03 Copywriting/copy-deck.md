# Week 01 Copy Deck — "Make it obvious."

**Copywriting Specialist** with Content Director and MD sign-off.
**Voice:** confident, sharp, warm, commercially aware. Plain English with Nigerian business texture. No hype words ("elevate", "unlock", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world"), no motivational filler.
**Placeholders to replace before publishing:** `[WhatsApp link]` (wa.me link with Prohub's business number) and `[Prohub IG handle]`. Agents never invent contact details.
