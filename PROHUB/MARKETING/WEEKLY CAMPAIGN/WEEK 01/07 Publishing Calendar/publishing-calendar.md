## Timing rationale (hypotheses to test, not facts)
- No historical Prohub account Insights were available, so times follow the audience's working pattern: **lunch (12:30–13:00)** and **evening wind-down (19:00–21:00)** for owners on Instagram and Facebook, and **before work (08:30–09:00)** on weekdays for LinkedIn decision-makers.
- Carousels (save- and send-heavy) go out in the evening, when people have time to swipe. Reels go at lunch and early evening.
- Saturday posts late morning. Sunday evening is when owners plan their week, which suits the conversion post.
- **After week 1:** export Instagram/Facebook "Most active times" and LinkedIn analytics, and move each slot to the observed peak.

## Daily routine (Social Media Manager)
- **Before each post:** final check of caption, hashtags (3–5 on IG), alt text, `[WhatsApp link]` live, keyword CTA spelt the same in art and caption.
- **First 60 minutes after each post:** reply to every comment and DM (scripts in `02 Content Strategy/lead-generation-strategy.md`).
- **21:30 daily:** log DMs and comments by keyword into `08 Reports/lead-tracker.csv`, and fill in the day's row in `08 Reports/performance-log.csv`.
