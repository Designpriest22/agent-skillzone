# Week 01 Content Strategy — "Make it obvious."

**Owner:** Managing Director · **Contributors:** Sales & Marketing, Content, Copywriting, Graphic Design, Video, Social Media
**Live window:** Mon 9 – Sun 15 Nov 2026 (WAT) · **Markets:** Abuja and Lagos first, international-standard presentation

---

## 1. The campaign idea

**Insight.** Nigerian customers aren't short of options. What they're short of is reasons to trust one. After a year in which 84% of adults surveyed ran into a scam (GASA 2026), buyers pick the business that is *obviously* credible, *obviously* right for them and *obviously* the one they remember. The best business in the category doesn't always win that choice.

**Platform line:** **Make it obvious.**

**Role for Prohub:** We're the team that makes the right choice obvious: in your identity, your content, your video, your website and your positioning.

Why this beats a generic "we offer branding" campaign:
- It's a **point of view**, not a service list, and every post can prove it.
- It gives the week **one thread**: Monday's logo test returns on Wednesday ("logo test, passed"), the trust argument on Thursday sets up Sunday's December offer, and every CTA is a keyword DM.
- It's **commercially aware**. It argues against wasted spend (boosting without positioning) at a time of 15.9% inflation and a 26.5% policy rate.

## 3. What the MD changed from the starting framework (and why)

| Day | Brief's starting point | Change | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "What makes a business memorable?" | Turned into a **test the viewer can run**: cover your logo | A question invites a shrug, a test invites action. Backed by Ehrenberg-Bass / Ipsos × JKR research. |
| 2 | Generic "branding mistakes" | **Seven Nigerian-specific leaks**, each with a visual piece of evidence ("Check your DM for price", 9-font flyer, dead link in bio) | Specific beats generic. Recognition drives sends, Instagram's strongest reach signal. |
| 3 | "Showcase our capabilities" | **Full fictional rebrand** (Ember & Yaji), clearly labelled | No client work is cleared for publishing. A fictional case shows the *whole* process (insight → identity → applications → feed) without borrowing anyone's work. |
| 4 | Marketing vs positioning | Added the **boost-but-no-sales symptom** and the **trust statistic** | Every Nigerian SME owner has boosted a post. The 84% stat makes "positioning" a sales issue, not theory. |
| 5 | List of services | **Problem → fix** mapping in the owner's own words | People don't search for "creative strategy". They say "we're busy but not growing." |
| 6 | "Creative industry conversation" | **Free Brand Read** (drop your handle) | A debate gets comments. A Brand Read gets comments from **business owners with a need**, i.e. leads. |
| 7 | "Ready to move forward?" | **December urgency + a specific, low-risk offer** (20-min Brand Direction Call) | Five to six weeks before the ₦396bn season is exactly when event, hospitality and retail brands commission work. |

## 4. CTA ladder: one keyword per post, so every enquiry can be traced

| Day | CTA | Keyword / action | Why this CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Run the test on your last five posts. Then DM us OBVIOUS." | **OBVIOUS** | Low commitment, curiosity-led |
| 2 | "Send this to whoever runs your brand. Or DM FIX with the leaks you spotted." | **FIX** + sends | Drives sends (reach) *and* self-qualified DMs |
| 3 | "Imagine what we'd do with a real one. Tell us what you're building." | **SHOWCASE** | Converts admiration into a project conversation |
| 4 | "Comment your one-line answer to 'Why you?'" | Comment (public) | Public engagement plus a visible list of owners who care about positioning |
| 5 | "DM us the number of the problem that sounds like you." | **BUILD** / slide number | Qualifies by need before the first reply |
| 6 | "Drop your business handle." | Comment handle | Builds the prospect list directly |
| 7 | "Book a 20-minute Brand Direction Call. DM DECEMBER." | **DECEMBER** | Specific, time-bound, high intent |

No CTA repeats. Keywords let the Social Media Manager tag every DM to its source post (see the lead-generation strategy).

## 5. Platform roles (adapted, not cross-posted)

| Platform | Role this week | Adaptation rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Main stage: Reels for reach, carousels for saves and sends, Stories daily for interaction | 3–5 hashtags; caption opens with the hook line; keyword CTA |

## 6. Creative direction

- **Look:** editorial, high-contrast and confident. Three grounds in rotation (ink #0C0C0D, warm paper #F3EFE8, Prohub orange #FD8D18) so the grid never repeats itself. Fine film grain on dark grounds for texture, no stock photography, no gradients-for-the-sake-of-it.
- **Brand:** Prohub's own vectorised logo and monogram (`prohub-os/assets/brand/prohub/`). The **orange swoosh from the logo is the campaign's mark of emphasis**, so Prohub uses its own distinctive asset all week, the same principle Day 1 teaches.
- **Type:** Montserrat ExtraBold for headlines (matches the geometric Prohub wordmark), Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for the editorial "meta" labels, Archivo Black for big numerals. All are already in Prohub's font kit.
- **Text discipline:** one idea per frame. Headline at most 9 words, support line at most 25.
- **Evidence over decoration:** every carousel slide carries a small illustration of the problem (comment threads, a chaotic flyer, a dead-link screen, a "boosted post" dashboard) instead of an icon.
- **Motion:** Prohub OS house styles. **Cinematic** (dark HUD, slamming type) for POV/urgency reels on Days 1 and 7, **Studio** (clean UI-motion, design-tool language) for craft and service reels on Days 3 and 5. Sound design on every cut; the music bed is Prohub's own promo track (rights confirmation required, see approvals).
