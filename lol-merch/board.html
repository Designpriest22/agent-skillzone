The concept
Fixture: DEC 13.
Venue: Eagle Square.

We treat the festival like a matchday. The LOL logo is worn like a club crest on the chest, and the date is worn like a squad number on the back. From across the crowd or on a phone screen, one thing reads first: DEC 13.

The language is football kit (crest, numbering, jock tag) built on premium streetwear blanks. Every colour comes from the official logo. Nothing is added that the logo doesn't already own.

Crest on the chestThe original logo, never redrawn, set on a black crest badge with a gold rule. One treatment on every garment and every colour.
13 on the backCustom chamfered numerals built like tackle twill: fill, gap, outline and an inner stitch line. DEC sits above in the same layers.
Matchday detailsA "LOL FEST 2026" nape print and an "Official Merchandise" jock tag at the hem. The collared polo keeps a clean back.

Fabric & decoration

ItemRecommendation
Tee260–280 gsm combed ring-spun cotton jersey, boxy drop shoulder, 2.5 cm rib neck, garment-dyed for Onyx and Ember. Crest: digital (DTF) print with a high-density clear top-coat. Number: layered tackle-twill appliqué with zig-zag stitch (premium) or layered HD plastisol print (volume).
Jersey160–180 gsm recycled-polyester micro-mesh, moisture-wicking. 2.5 cm contrast rib binding with a knitted accent stripe. Side stripes knitted in. Number: cut heat-transfer TPU in 3 layers. Chalk body can be fully sublimated.
Polo220 gsm cotton-poly piqué, flat-knit collar and cuffs with tipping, 2-button placket with branded buttons. Back: clean, nape print only.
Sleeveless240–260 gsm combed cotton jersey, boxy cropped fit. Square cut-away armholes finished with a turned, twin-needle-stitched band, and a 2.5 cm rib crew neck. Crest: DTF + HD top-coat. Number: tackle twill or layered HD print.
CrestDye-sublimated woven badge with a merrowed gold border, heat-sealed then edge-stitched. This keeps the logo's 3D gradients exactly as supplied; thread embroidery cannot reproduce them.
FinishingTagless printed neck label (size + "Official Merchandise"), woven jock tag, wash-tested for shrinkage and number lift (ISO 6330), size run XS–3XL.
Logo rule. The LOL logo is used exactly as supplied: it is cut from the official artwork, never redrawn, recoloured or re-proportioned. It always sits on the black crest so the cream "Music Festival" line and the brackets stay legible on light garments. For production, request the master vector or high-resolution file from the festival. The mockups use the logo as cut from the flyer.
