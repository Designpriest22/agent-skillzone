# Launch strategy

Tiers: 1 major (4–8 weeks prep, all channels) · 2 feature · 3 update · 4 changelog.
Positioning: For [audience] who [need], [product] is the [category] that [benefit]. Unlike [alternative], we [differentiator].

Phases
1. Pre-launch (weeks −8 to −1): positioning, assets, waitlist, beta, community warming, tracking verified, support briefed.
2. Launch day: time-boxed checklist (T−2h checks, T0 publish + send, T+2h metrics, EOD report).
3. Post-launch 30 days: follow-up content, testimonials, retargeting, funnel review, playbook write-up.

ORB channels: Owned (email, site, in-app) · Rented (paid, sponsorships) · Borrowed (press, influencers, communities, partners).

Metrics: waitlist size, launch-day visitors (5× normal), signup conversion 5–15%, activation of launch cohort, 30-day signups 2–5× baseline.
