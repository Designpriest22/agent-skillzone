# Grants

## Before drafting
Confirm funder + programme, project idea, budget envelope, team. If unknown, state assumptions at the top.

## Funder fit (score 7 dimensions; < 65/100 = reconsider)
Topic · mechanism (R&D / services / scale-up) · stage · geography · team profile · award size · competitive density.

## Write to the funder's mental model
| Funder | Emphasises |
|---|---|
| Government / development | Measurable public impact, jobs, inclusion, compliance |
| Foundation | Mission fit + measurable outcomes |
| Corporate | Relevance to sponsor |
| Innovation / SBIR-type | Commercial path + technical risk |

## Heilmeier test — every proposal must answer crisply
1 What are you doing? 2 How is it done today, limits? 3 What's new, why will it work? 4 Who cares, what changes?
5 Risks + mitigation? 6 Cost + duration? 7 Mid-term and final outcomes?

## Budget rules
Every line ties to an activity and a deliverable. Show unit × quantity × rate. Include contingency (5–10%),
monitoring & evaluation (5–10%), and admin within funder norms. Budget must match scope — mismatch is a red flag.

## Audit checklist
Fit assumptions · structure vs template · problem specific and data-backed · approach novel · budget justified ·
team credentials match scope · risks named · no jargon or vague claims · KPIs measurable with baselines.
