# Pitch deck

Iron law: 10–15 slides, one idea per slide, ≤ 30–40 words per slide, one chart per slide.
Order: Title · Problem (data-backed) · Solution · Product · Market (TAM/SAM/SOM + method) · Business model · Traction ·
Competition (2×2 map, not a feature table) · Team · Financials (3-year, assumptions) · The Ask (amount, use of funds, milestones) · Contact.
Lead with the strongest proof. Never claim "no competition". Projections need a bottom-up basis.
Keep a presenting version (big visuals) and a read-ahead version (self-explanatory).
