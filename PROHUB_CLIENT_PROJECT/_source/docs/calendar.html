04 · Social media strategy · Launch week 09–15 November 2026

Seven days, one conversion arc.

Each post sets up the next: awareness (the reset) → interest (the headliner) → consideration (the menu) → engagement (the debate) → proof (the recap) → conversion (the order) → advocacy (the table). Platforms: Instagram and Facebook (Meta Business Suite cross-post), TikTok and WhatsApp. Supporting stories run 3–5 times daily. Prices, venue and price-step dates are client-to-confirm fields.
