4 · Workflow & approvals

  1. Brief — weekly content plan shared every Thursday for the following week.
  2. Create — Prohub produces designs, copy and video to the system.
  3. Review — one consolidated round of client feedback.
  4. Revise — up to two revision rounds per asset.
  5. Approve & schedule — approved assets are scheduled; nothing publishes without approval.
  6. Measure — weekly report every Monday; plan adjusted to what sells.
Turnaround commitments
  • Client approvals: within 24 hours (one named decision-maker)
  • Prohub reactive posts (e.g. artist confirmations): same day
  • Event-day highlights: within 24 hours
  • Communication: one shared WhatsApp channel + weekly 30-min call

7 · Reporting

Every Monday: tickets sold by tier and date; sales by channel, creator code and post; cost per ticket; reach, saves, shares, link taps; WhatsApp opt-ins; what we’re changing this week. Final results report within 7 days of the event, including recommendations for the next edition.

8 · Assumptions & exclusions

  • Fees are quoted per module after scope confirmation; this document contains no pricing.
  • Media budget, creator fees, ticketing fees and printing are paid by the client and are separate from Prohub fees.
  • Concept designs in this proposal are proposed visual directions; final artwork follows confirmation of event facts.
  • Artist imagery depends on client-supplied press photos and usage permissions.
  • Concept photography for the campaign (food, crowd, flash documentary) will be produced or licensed during production.
  • Prohub does not publish event facts (venue, price, line-up) without written client confirmation.
