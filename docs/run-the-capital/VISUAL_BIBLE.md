# RUN THE CAPITAL — Visual Bible (canon)

Built with the `higgsfield-character-design` method: premise → world → style sheet → hand-off. Every generation prompt injects **Visual DNA** and **Forbidden List** verbatim.

## Premise
Respect in the capital is earned on the street, not handed down from the hill.

- **Counter:** some doors in Abuja stay shut no matter how hard you fight.
- **Promise:** *swagger*, the thrill of a whole district chanting your name.

## World sheet
| Dimension | Canon |
|---|---|
| Physical | Planned city of wide roads and roundabouts, with Aso Rock always on the skyline. Wuse II plazas, Aminu Kano Crescent neon strip, Garki market lanes, Area 1 estates, Jabi Lake waterfront. |
| Social | Power flows from "connection" (who you know) and "area" (where you're from). |
| Economic | Hustle economy: touts, POS stands, keke routes, nightlife, protection money. |
| Ideological | Loyalty to your area is sacred. Disgracing your people is shameful. Showing off is mandatory. |
| Historical | Everyone remembers when their area was "just bush". Everyone has an uncle who "built this city". |
| Sensory | Suya smoke, generator hum, harmattan dust, keke horns, amapiano from three speakers at once. |

## Visual DNA (inject verbatim)
> Hand-drawn comic-book fighting-game art in the lineage of Streets of Rage 4: bold confident hand-inked outlines of varied weight, flat cel shading with two to three tones, hard rim light, painterly flat-colour backgrounds with visible brush texture. Heroic proportions about 1:6.5 with big hands and feet, strong readable silhouettes, dramatic poses. Palette: ink #14161C, night teal #0F2A33, panel teal #12333B, fire orange #FF8A1F, TCM red #E8452C, gold #FFC93C, Naija green #00A862, concrete #C9C2B6. Night scenes take sodium-orange street light and cyan bounce light. The Aminu Kano strip alone adds neon magenta #FF3FA4.

## Forbidden list (inject verbatim)
> No photorealism, no 3D render or plastic look, no glossy airbrushed AI sheen, no pixel art, no generic cyberpunk city, no gibberish text or fake signage letters, no extra fingers or limbs, no gore, no sexualised poses or framing, no guns pointed at the viewer, no logos of real brands, no pure-black empty backgrounds in environments.

## Construction laws
- **Fighter portraits:** waist-up, three-quarter, facing right, on flat key colour. The background is **magenta #FF00FF**, or **green #00FF00** for fighters wearing pink or magenta. Keyed locally.
- **Real artists** (Odumodublvck, DJ Romie, PsychoYP, Obey The Hypeman): the photo reference is the face lock. Signature wardrobe is kept, and nothing is invented that the artist wouldn't wear. Publishing needs **written consent**.
- **Stage backgrounds:** 21:9, eye-level side view, the lower third an empty walkable street (no people, no cars in the walk zone), landmarks in the upper half.
- **In-game sprites:** same DNA, pure side profile facing right, generated as image-to-video "treadmill" clips, then keyed and packed (scenario side-view kit method).
