Proposed project

Run The Capital

A free browser game where players fight through Abuja’s real districts as the city’s own artists. It plays on any phone with no download, and every score is verified on TCM’s server. A working build exists today; the grant takes it to a full public launch.

14fighters, including Odumodublvck and DJ Romie
6/15stages playable today
100%of rewarded runs replayed on the server
19/19automated end-to-end checks passing

Objectives

Put Abuja on the digital map

Complete all 5 districts and 15 stages, set in real Abuja places and culture.

Grow young creative talent

Train 30 young Abuja artists, animators and coders in the Capital Game Lab.

Build a lasting product

Fund running costs from brand placements and cosmetics, never pay-to-win.

Decide with data

Use live player analytics to guide every update and report to the funder.

A national market, won from the capital

Nigeria has Africa’s second-largest games market and almost all of it plays on mobile. No mainstream game tells Abuja’s story. TCM brings the content, the artists and the crowds.
