Project summary

Run The Capital

Grant request

₦50,000,00012 months

A free browser fighting game set in real Abuja. Players fight through the city’s districts as Abuja’s own artists, including Odumodublvck and DJ Romie. It runs on any phone with no download.

The opportunity

46.5 million Nigerians play games, about 90% of them on mobile. No mainstream game tells Abuja’s story.

Where it stands

A working build already exists: 14 fighters, 6 playable stages, player profiles, leaderboards, secure payments and live player analytics.

What the grant funds

  • Completing the game: 24 stages across Abuja
  • Animation, art and music by Abuja creatives
  • Training 60 young Abuja creatives in game production
  • Public launch at a TCM event

How it sustains itself

  • In-game brand placements
  • Event tie-ins and tournaments
  • Cosmetic items, never pay-to-win
  • Break-even in year two

12-month targets

130kplayers
90kregistered profiles
60youths trained
18paid creative contracts
24stages of real Abuja

Who delivers it

The Capital Movement (TCM), Abuja’s culture platform since 2016: 100+ events, 50,000+ people and 20+ brand partners.
