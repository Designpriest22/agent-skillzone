Project summary

Run The Capital

Grant request

₦50,000,00012 months

A free browser fighting game set in real Abuja, starring Odumodu Blvck. Players fight through the city’s districts as Odumodu, calling in a crew of Abuja characters for backup. It runs on any phone with no download.

The opportunity

46.5 million Nigerians play games, about 90% of them on mobile. No mainstream game tells Abuja’s story.

Where it stands

A working build already exists: Odumodu Blvck as the lead, 13 crew characters, 6 playable stages, player profiles, leaderboards, secure payments and live player analytics.

What the grant funds

  • Completing the game: 24 stages across Abuja
  • Animation, art and music by Abuja creatives
  • Training 60 young Abuja creatives in game production
  • Public launch at a TCM event

How it sustains itself

  • In-game brand placements
  • Event tie-ins and tournaments
  • Cosmetic items, never pay-to-win
  • Target: 90,000 daily players by year two, earning ₦797M a year

Impact on Abuja

Abuja on the world’s screens. The capital’s streets, neighbourhoods and artists reach players across Nigeria and the diaspora.

A home-grown games industry. Abuja gains its own production pipeline for game art, animation, music and code.

Jobs and skills for young people. Paid work for local creatives and training that leads to digital careers.

A new image of the capital. Abuja seen as a home of culture and creativity, not only government.
